What a privilege it is<br>
to be gone<br>
to be back<br>
and be somewhere new in each
<br><br>
Returning from an icy kingdom<br>
where a piece of my mind was left behind<br>
part of me stays there captive in its beauty<br>
in the snowy crust, in the green pooled gardens, <br>
in the dense cold blue glow<br>
Part of me stays above it<br>
within the colors in the sky<br>
often soft in shades of blue, orange, pink<br>
that transition from one to the other in<br>
gentle, indistinguishable fronts<br>
Yet more often in a cast of white or gray <br>
in a brightness enhanced by the white surface below<br>
Imagine that, light amplified above and below,<br>
from sun to cloud to ice to cloud to then be absorbed by my eyes<br>
but perhaps to ice had my eyes not been here<br>
It is true, my being here changes this place<br>
and it changes me in turn
